Vector Embeddings for Skills: Use a model like text-embedding-3-small or a specialized HuggingFace model to map "Strategic Leader" to "Executive Oversight." This helps candidates who use different terminology still find the right roles.

Success Profiling: Instead of matching just to a static job description (JD), allow recruiters to upload the "ideal profile" of a current top performer. The AI can then flag candidates with similar career trajectories or soft skill patterns.

Gap Analysis: If a candidate is an 80% match, show them exactly what 20% they are missing (e.g., "You need AWS Certification for this role") and link to a learning resource.

Match Scoring Transparency: Avoid the "Black Box" problem. Provide a breakdown of why someone was a 95% match (e.g., "5 years experience in React (High Impact), 3 years in Node.js (Medium Impact)").
